────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. OBJECTIVE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This test case validates the capability of the Archer 750 system to detect
and classify ESD Damage defects during post-oxidation inspection
at the 22nm technology node. The test is conducted at Singapore Fab 7 to ensure
manufacturing quality standards are met for 450mm wafer production.

The primary objective is to verify that the inspection system meets the required
detection sensitivity and classification accuracy for ESD Damage defects
that are critical to yield at the 22nm node. This test case is part of
the ongoing qualification program for the Archer 750 platform.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. SCOPE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- Product Line: Archer 750
- Inspection Type: Bare Wafer Inspection
- Target Defect: ESD Damage
- Process Step: Post-Oxidation
- Wafer Size: 450mm
- Technology Node: 22nm
- Fab Location: Singapore Fab 7

This test case applies to all Archer 750 systems deployed at Singapore Fab 7
for post-oxidation monitoring in the 22nm manufacturing line.
Results from this test are used to establish baseline performance metrics
and to qualify new recipe configurations.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. TEST CONFIGURATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

System:                Archer 750
Software Version:      v12.3.762
Recipe:                BARE_WAFER_INSPECTION_22nm_POST_OXIDATION
Illumination Mode:     Darkfield
Scan Speed:            Low (147 mm/s)
Pixel Size:            0.5μm
Edge Exclusion:        3mm
Sampling Plan:         5-point sampling

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. TEST PROCEDURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Power on the Archer 750 system and allow 30-minute warm-up period.
2. Load the calibration wafer and run the standard calibration routine.
3. Verify calibration results are within specification (±2% tolerance).
4. Load the test wafer onto the stage using the automated handler.
5. Select the 'Bare Wafer Inspection' recipe from the recipe library.
6. Configure detection sensitivity to target ESD Damage defects.
7. Set scan parameters: full wafer scan with edge exclusion of 3mm.
8. Initiate the inspection run and monitor real-time defect map.
9. Upon completion, review the defect density map and classification results.
10. Export results to the factory automation system (SECS/GEM interface).
11. Compare results against the golden reference baseline.
12. Document any anomalies and generate the summary report.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- Defect detection sensitivity must meet 22nm node requirements
- False positive rate shall not exceed 5% of total detected events
- Scan throughput must be ≥ 76 wafers per hour
- Classification accuracy shall be ≥ 95% for known defect types
- Repeatability (3-sigma) shall be within ±0.7% for Bare Wafer Inspection
- System uptime during test shall be ≥ 98%
- All results must be traceable through the MES integration
- Measurement correlation with reference tool shall be ≥ 0.97 R²

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. OBSERVATIONS AND FINDINGS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

During the execution of this test case, the following observations were noted:

a) The Archer 750 demonstrated acceptable performance
   in detecting ESD Damage at the 22nm node.

b) The capture rate exceeded the minimum threshold, confirming system readiness.

c) Nuisance rate is within acceptable limits.

d) Edge-zone defect detection showed improved performance compared to previous revision.

e) SECS/GEM communication was verified with zero packet loss during data transfer.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS (if applicable)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


The following corrective actions are recommended:

1. Review edge-zone detection parameters for optimization.
2. Schedule follow-up test after recipe optimization.
3. Monitor performance over next 30 production days.
