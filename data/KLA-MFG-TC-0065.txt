────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. OBJECTIVE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This test case validates the capability of the eDR-7100 system to detect
and classify Crystal Originated Particles (COP) defects during post-deposition inspection
at the 22nm technology node. The test is conducted at Dresden Fab 3 to ensure
manufacturing quality standards are met for 200mm wafer production.

The primary objective is to verify that the inspection system meets the required
detection sensitivity and classification accuracy for Crystal Originated Particles (COP) defects
that are critical to yield at the 22nm node. This test case is part of
the ongoing qualification program for the eDR-7100 platform.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. SCOPE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- Product Line: eDR-7100
- Inspection Type: Patterned Wafer Inspection
- Target Defect: Crystal Originated Particles (COP)
- Process Step: Post-Deposition
- Wafer Size: 200mm
- Technology Node: 22nm
- Fab Location: Dresden Fab 3

This test case applies to all eDR-7100 systems deployed at Dresden Fab 3
for post-deposition monitoring in the 22nm manufacturing line.
Results from this test are used to establish baseline performance metrics
and to qualify new recipe configurations.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. TEST CONFIGURATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

System:                eDR-7100
Software Version:      v9.1.203
Recipe:                PATTERNED_WAFER_INSPECTION_22nm_POST_DEPOSITION
Illumination Mode:     Darkfield
Scan Speed:            Low (162 mm/s)
Pixel Size:            0.25μm
Edge Exclusion:        4mm
Sampling Plan:         9-point sampling

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. TEST PROCEDURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Power on the eDR-7100 system and allow 30-minute warm-up period.
2. Load the calibration wafer and run the standard calibration routine.
3. Verify calibration results are within specification (±2% tolerance).
4. Load the test wafer onto the stage using the automated handler.
5. Select the 'Patterned Wafer Inspection' recipe from the recipe library.
6. Configure detection sensitivity to target Crystal Originated Particles (COP) defects.
7. Set scan parameters: full wafer scan with edge exclusion of 3mm.
8. Initiate the inspection run and monitor real-time defect map.
9. Upon completion, review the defect density map and classification results.
10. Export results to the factory automation system (SECS/GEM interface).
11. Compare results against the golden reference baseline.
12. Document any anomalies and generate the summary report.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- Defect detection sensitivity must meet 22nm node requirements
- False positive rate shall not exceed 5% of total detected events
- Scan throughput must be ≥ 49 wafers per hour
- Classification accuracy shall be ≥ 91% for known defect types
- Repeatability (3-sigma) shall be within ±0.6% for Patterned Wafer Inspection
- System uptime during test shall be ≥ 98%
- All results must be traceable through the MES integration
- Measurement correlation with reference tool shall be ≥ 0.95 R²

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. OBSERVATIONS AND FINDINGS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

During the execution of this test case, the following observations were noted:

a) The eDR-7100 demonstrated acceptable performance
   in detecting Crystal Originated Particles (COP) at the 22nm node.

b) The capture rate requires optimization of the detection algorithm.

c) Nuisance rate is within acceptable limits.

d) No significant drift was observed during the test duration.

e) Recipe portability between systems at this fab was confirmed.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS (if applicable)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


The following corrective actions are recommended:

1. Review edge-zone detection parameters for optimization.
2. Schedule follow-up test after recipe optimization.
3. Monitor performance over next 30 production days.
